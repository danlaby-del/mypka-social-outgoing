  None of this is abstract for an athlete.

  A hitter deciding whether to swing has a few hundred milliseconds to commit or not commit to a motor response. A goalkeeper reading a penalty taker's hips has to resist looking at the ball too early. A defensive back has to hold his eyes on the quarterback's shoulders and not bite on the pump fake. Every one of those is an antisaccade problem wearing a different uniform.

  I ran this same battery twice in one day and landed consistent numbers both times, an encouraging early sign, though real proof of reproducibility still needs formal test-retest validation, more sessions, and comparison against a validated tracker, not just two good runs.

  That's why I'm training on this myself, on a fixed cadence, and publishing what the numbers actually look like along the way. Not a cherry-picked highlight. Not a claim without a dataset behind it.
