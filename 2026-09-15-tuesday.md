facebook_text: |
  Everything in your training assumes the same thing: cleaner is better. Cleaner mechanics, cleaner reps, cleaner video, cleaner data.

  For most of what we do, that is correct. For the visual system, it is sometimes exactly backwards.

  There is a figure in Chapter 10 of Eye of the Champion I keep returning to. A greyscale photograph of a flag, then six copies with steadily increasing random noise laid over the top. In the first, the contrast is so low you see nothing at all. By the second and third, the noise has made the flag emerge and you can identify it. By the sixth, the field is a mess and the flag is gone again.

  The target never changed. The only thing that changed was the noise.

  Too little and it is invisible. Too much and it is buried. In between sits a dose at which you see something you genuinely could not see before.

  That is stochastic resonance, and this week's episode is about what it means for athletes.
