facebook_text: |
  Here's the trap I've spent a good part of Eye of the Champion warning about: mistaking a clean, passive vision screening for a measurement of how an athlete actually sees in competition.

  Tracking a moving dot on a screen is a real measurement of something. It is not automatically a measurement of how that same athlete tracks a 90-mile-an-hour fastball they're about to swing at.

  I saw this firsthand more than a decade ago, eye-tracking MLB batters. When we asked them to simply call ball or strike, their eye movements, even their head positions, looked nothing like when they actually swung. From that point on, we insisted on live pitching and a real swing to get an accurate read.

  Passive drills and screen-based tools aren't wasted. They're just not the finish line.

  See this week's SPORTS VISION RADIO podcast for a deeper dive.
