  Here's the mechanism behind this week's episode.

  A saccade is the fast, ballistic jump your eyes make shifting gaze from one point to another. Smooth pursuit is the slower, continuous glide that follows a moving target. Both are measurable down to the millisecond.

  The piece almost nobody has heard of is antisaccadic inhibitory control. A cue flashes to one side, your instinct is to look straight at it, and the task is to suppress that reflex and look the opposite way on purpose. It's a real, measurable readout of your brain overriding a reflex with a decision, under time pressure.

  I couldn't find a single consumer vision-training platform that actually measures a true antisaccade response rather than a button click standing in for one. So I built the real thing myself.

  → sportsvision.nyc
